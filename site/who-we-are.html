Obras Cullera was founded on a simple conviction: the countries that most need infrastructure deserve the same engineering standard, the same governance and the same after-care as the countries that already have it.

Our story

From a Valencian
coastal town to
twelve countries

The group began in Cullera as a civil-works contractor on the Valencian coast — sea walls, municipal roads, water networks. The discipline of that first decade, working for town halls with small budgets and long memories, became the operating culture of everything that followed.

Our first international mandate took us to West Africa. What we found was not a shortage of ambition or of capital, but a shortage of partners willing to remain present after commissioning. We built the company around that gap.

Today Obras Cullera operates as an integrated development group: we help structure the project, arrange the financing, build the asset, and train the people who will operate it — and we hold ourselves to the maintenance outcome, not only the construction milestone.

Values & governance

Six commitments
we can be held to

These are not aspirations. Each one is written into our contracts, audited annually and reported publicly.

Procurement integrity

Open tender records, declared intermediaries, and a zero-tolerance facilitation-payment policy applied without exception in every jurisdiction.

Engineering discipline

Independent design review before mobilisation, and a lifecycle cost model attached to every technical proposal we submit.

Local content

A contractual floor for local employment, local procurement and technology transfer on every mandate, measured quarterly.

Environmental stewardship

Impact assessment aligned to IFC Performance Standards, with restoration obligations funded from the construction budget.

Safety without exception

One safety standard across the group, applied to subcontractors on the same terms as to our own crews.

Presence after handover

Every delivery contract carries an operations-and-maintenance obligation, a spare-parts plan and a trained local team.
