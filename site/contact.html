Careers

We recruit engineers, quantity surveyors, environmental and social specialists and finance professionals across all three regions. Applications from nationals of our host countries are prioritised at every grade.

Suppliers & partners

Local suppliers can register for pre-qualification at any time. Registration is free, requires no intermediary, and no payment is ever requested in exchange for consideration.

Suspected irregularity? Our whistleblowing channel is operated externally and is available in Spanish, French, English and Portuguese, with protection guarantees for the reporting party.
