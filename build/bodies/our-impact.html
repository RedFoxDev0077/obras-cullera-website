An infrastructure project is judged twice: once at inauguration, and once a decade later. We build and report for the second judgement.

Our framework

Four obligations,
attached to every
contract we sign

Impact is not a communications exercise. On each mandate we negotiate four measurable obligations into the contract itself, and we report against them quarterly to the client and annually in public.

Where we miss a target, the review says so. A framework that only ever produces good news is not a framework.

Read the latest review

01 — Local employment & skills

A contractual floor for national employment at every grade, apprenticeship places tied to the construction programme, and a named successor for each expatriate role.

02 — Local procurement

A minimum share of spend with suppliers registered in the host country, with payment terms short enough that small firms can actually participate.

03 — Environment & restoration

Assessment aligned to IFC Performance Standards, restoration funded from the construction budget rather than deferred, and monitored for three years after handover.

04 — Community & grievance

A published grievance mechanism with response deadlines, community liaison officers resident on site, and compensation records open to independent audit.

Programmes

Beyond the
site boundary

Four standing programmes run alongside our construction activity, funded as a fixed percentage of contract value rather than from discretionary budgets.

01

Site academies

Certified training in surveying, plant operation, electrical and civil trades, delivered on site and recognised by the national vocational authority.

02

Supplier development

Pre-qualification support, safety training and advance payment facilities so that local firms can bid for our packages on realistic terms.

03

Water & health points

Boreholes, standpipes and clinic upgrades in the communities along our corridors, handed to municipal ownership with a maintenance endowment.

04

Engineering scholarships

University places and paid internships for students from the regions where we build, with a guaranteed first-role offer on graduation.

Governance

Compliance as
an operating system

Our compliance function reports to the board, not to the commercial line. It can stop a tender, and it has.

Anti-corruption

Zero-tolerance facilitation-payment policy, declared intermediaries, and mandatory counterparty due diligence before any commitment.

Whistleblowing

An externally operated channel available in Spanish, French, English and Portuguese, with protection guarantees and published case statistics.

Certified systems

ISO 9001 quality, ISO 14001 environment and ISO 45001 occupational health and safety, audited across all operating territories.
