How we want
to generate impact

Obras Cullera does not simply look for business. We want to structure opportunities that make economic, territorial and social sense — and we would rather state the criteria we work to than claim results we have not yet produced.

Our criteria

Criteria we work to,
not results we
have not produced.

An operation that works only for the party promoting it rarely survives. The ones that last are those where the host country, the local companies and the partners involved all have a reason to keep them going.

These four principles guide how we assess and structure an opportunity. As real operations advance, we will report against them with figures we can evidence.

See how we work

01 — Local development

Favouring the involvement of local companies, professionals and capabilities in each operation.

02 — Transfer of knowledge

Building knowledge and training into projects wherever it is possible to do so.

03 — Long-term viability

Developing operations that can be sustained economically and operationally once they are running.

04 — Governance and transparency

Defining responsibilities, decision-making processes and clear follow-up mechanisms from the outset.

The test we apply

One question before
anything is published

Are we saying that Obras Cullera has already done something, or explaining what Obras Cullera is prepared to develop? Where real, evidenced experience exists we will describe it. Until then we speak in terms of development, structuring, coordination and the capacity to assemble teams.

This section deliberately contains no figures for jobs, population reached, local procurement, communities or ESG indicators. Those will be published only when they come from real, documented operations of Obras Cullera.
