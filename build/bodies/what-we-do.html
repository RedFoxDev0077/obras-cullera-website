How we work

One opportunity.
One structured process.

From initial identification through to the development of the operation, we keep an overall view and coordinate the capabilities needed at each stage.

01

Identification

We detect a need, an investment opportunity or a possibility for business development.

02

Analysis

We study the context, initial viability, participants, risks and possible structures for the operation.

03

Connection

We identify and bring in the technical, commercial, institutional, financial or operational partners required.

04

Structuring

We define responsibilities, the participation model, agreements and a roadmap for developing the operation.

05

Development

We coordinate negotiation, implementation and execution alongside the specialists and partners responsible for each discipline.

06

Follow-up

We accompany the operation as it develops, coordinate the parties and maintain an overall view of objectives, commitments and progress.

Structure and clarity

International operations can fail not for lack of opportunity, but because of an inadequate structure, unclear responsibilities or interests that are not properly aligned.

Obras Cullera works from the outset to order the participants, responsibilities, objectives and follow-up mechanisms of each operation.
