Obras Cullera is a Spanish business and development company. We identify opportunities, connect capabilities, structure operations and accompany their development.

Who we are

We do not execute
every discipline.
We make the operation
possible.

Obras Cullera is not an engineering firm, an EPC contractor, a financial institution, an airline or a consultancy specialising in every discipline.

It is a business platform for identifying, structuring, developing and coordinating opportunities and projects — bringing together, for each operation, the technical, commercial, financial, institutional or specialist operating partners that it requires.

Our value does not lie in performing every discipline internally, but in making the operation possible and organising the parties needed to develop it.

How we work

Commitments we
can be held to

These describe how we intend to work on every operation, not results we are claiming to have already produced.

Integrity

Declared intermediaries and a zero-tolerance policy on facilitation payments, applied without exception in every jurisdiction.

A clear structure

Participants, responsibilities, objectives and timetable defined from the start of each operation.

Local involvement

Favouring the participation of local companies, professionals and capabilities wherever the operation allows.

The right specialists

Each discipline carried out by those qualified and licensed to carry it out, coordinated by us.

Confidentiality

Information shared by counterparties is treated confidentially, under a non-disclosure agreement where required.

What we say we are

We describe capabilities we can deliver and experience we can evidence — and nothing beyond that.
