Commercial and coordination solutions for private and executive flights, arranged through licensed and specialised air operators, for regional and intercontinental travel.

The business line

Freight has a
schedule. So do
the people who
sign for it.

The same operations that move goods move executives, delegations, technical teams and institutional travellers — often to places and on timings that scheduled aviation does not serve well.

AZAN arranges that travel as a commercial and coordination service: understanding the requirement, identifying the right operator and aircraft category, structuring the commercial terms and coordinating the movement from end to end.

The flight itself is always performed by a duly licensed air operator. AZAN does not operate aircraft and does not hold itself out as an air carrier.

Solutions

What we
arrange

Each requirement is matched to the operator and the aircraft category it actually needs, not to a fixed fleet.

01 — Private and executive flights

Individual flights for executives, owners and private travellers, arranged through licensed operators.

02 — Corporate and group movements

Teams, delegations and technical personnel travelling together, on timings the operation dictates.

03 — Institutional travel

Travel linked to official missions, negotiations and site visits, with the discretion such movements require.

04 — Operator selection

Identifying the appropriate operator and aircraft category for the route, the payload and the conditions.

05 — Route and permit coordination

Coordinating itineraries, handling and the clearances the route requires, together with the operator and its agents.

06 — Ground connection

Linking the flight with ground movements, so arrival at the airport is not the end of the journey.

How a request is handled

From request
to departure

01

The requirement

Route, dates, number of passengers, baggage or payload, and any condition the trip imposes.

02

Operator and aircraft

We identify licensed operators able to serve the route and the aircraft category appropriate to it.

03

Commercial terms

A clear proposal: what is included, what is not, and which party is responsible for each element.

04

Coordination

Itinerary, handling, clearances and ground connections coordinated with the operator before departure.

05

The flight

Performed by the licensed air operator, under its own certificate and its own operational responsibility.

AZAN Global Logistics is not an air operator and does not own or operate aircraft. All flights are performed by duly licensed and authorised air operators, under their own certificates.
