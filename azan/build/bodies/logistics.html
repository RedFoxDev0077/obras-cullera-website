The business line

A load does not
stop at the
border post.

Between a container landed at a port and a delivery in an inland city there are carriers, agents, customs posts, authorities and documents in more than one country. Each of them can stop the load.

AZAN builds the operation so that they do not. We define the route and the contractual framework, select and coordinate the operators, and remain the single party answerable for the movement.

The transport itself is performed by the operating structures of the group or by licensed carriers in each country. Our work is the design, the coordination and the responsibility.

Capabilities

What we
take on

Each capability can be contracted on its own or as part of a complete corridor operation.

01 — Cross-border road freight

Structuring and coordination of road movements between countries, including the border crossings on the route.

02 — Port and terminal connection

Connecting maritime arrivals and departures with inland transport, in coordination with port agents and terminal operators.

03 — Corridor design

Designing a repeatable route: stages, operators, transit points, documentation and the responsibilities of each party.

04 — Customs and documentation

Coordination with customs brokers and authorities so the paperwork travels with the goods rather than behind them.

05 — Operator selection

Identifying and contracting carriers and local specialists holding the authorisations their activity requires.

06 — Project and industrial cargo

Movements tied to works, equipment and industrial supply, where the schedule matters as much as the route.
