A Bahrain company
built to cross borders

AZAN Global Logistics W.L.L. structures and coordinates international logistics and mobility operations, with the Kingdom of Bahrain as its corporate base and Africa as its first territory of implementation.

The company

We structure
the operation.
Operators run it.

AZAN Global Logistics W.L.L. is constituted in the Kingdom of Bahrain. Bahrain is the corporate hub of the group: the place from which contracts are signed, operations are coordinated, commercial relations are held and projects are managed.

The physical side of an operation — vehicles, drivers, aircraft, permits, terminals — is carried out by the corresponding operating structures, subsidiaries or duly authorised partners in each country.

That division of roles is deliberate. It allows the company to work in several jurisdictions at the same time without diluting responsibility: whoever the operators are, the client has one counterparty.

How the group is organised

One hub,
local execution

Bahrain Corporate hub Contracting, coordination, commercial relations and management of the projects of the group.
In each country Operating structures Subsidiaries and local structures through which operations are physically carried out.
Alongside us Authorised partners Carriers, air operators, port agents and specialists holding the licences their activity requires.
For the client A single point of contact One counterparty, one plan and one consolidated view of the operation from end to end.

How we work

Principles we
do not negotiate

International logistics runs on trust between parties who may never meet. These are the terms on which we ask for it.

Clarity of role

We state precisely what AZAN does and what the operators do, in every quotation and in every contract.

Regulatory respect

Transport, customs and air activity are regulated. We work only with parties holding the authorisations their activity requires.

Integrity

Declared intermediaries and no facilitation payments, applied without exception in every jurisdiction in which we operate.

Local contribution

Wherever an operation allows it, we work with local companies, drivers, agents and professionals.

Confidentiality

Routes, volumes, rates and counterparties belong to our clients. We publish nothing they have not released.

Only what is real

We distinguish plainly between projects in development and operations already running. This site follows the same rule.

For the avoidance of doubt

What AZAN
is not

AZAN Global Logistics is not a haulier with its own fleet, and it is not an air operator. We do not own the trucks or the aircraft that carry out an operation, and we do not present ourselves as doing so.

What we provide is the structure around the movement: the contractual framework, the commercial solution, the coordination between the parties, and responsibility for the operation as a whole.

Corporate, registration and licensing details will be published on this site once they are confirmed and can be evidenced.
