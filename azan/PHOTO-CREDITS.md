## Generated images

The four accordion figures on the home page - `az-boardroom`,
`az-route-planning`, `az-warehouse` and `az-control-room` - are AI-generated
to the brief in `image-prompts.md`. They are interiors, so they carry no
location claim, and no third party holds rights in them.

## Rules applied when selecting

- No shipping line, airline or operator livery prominent in frame, so nothing
  implies a commercial relationship AZAN does not have. One strong port
  photograph was rejected for being covered in Maersk branding.
- No flags, border signage or readable number plates.
- Tropical Gulf of Guinea vegetation for the corridor images, not the dry
  savannah of the first round, which read to the client as the wrong region.
